Diabetes management system

MyGlooco is a mobile app that allows diabetics to have better control over their diabetes by helping to track blood sugar, building healthy habits and managing medication.

The Problem

Currently, individuals living with diabetes are facing a myriad of challenges, most of which are related to their current help systems.
Some rely on manual record keeping for their blood sugar level which has proven to be cumbersome, and others have little to no information on dietary planning, as well as exercise planning, which hinders their overall quality of life.

Challenges faced

  • Difficulty in Blood Sugar Tracking

    Many individuals rely on manual record keeping for glucose monitoring, which can be cumbersome and prone to errors.

  • Managing Medication

    Keeping track of multiple medications can be challenging, especially for older adults or individuals with many health conditions.

  • Regulating Diet Portions

    A lot of people struggle with controlling their meal portions and understanding how different foods affect their blood sugar levels.

  • Fitness Tracking

    Tracking physical activity and understanding its impact on glucose levels can be difficult.

  • Proximity to Healthcare Providers

    Access to healthcare providers for regular check-ups and consultations can be a challenge, especially for individuals living in remote areas.

The Solution

MyGlooco is therefore a mobile app built to monitor blood sugar, set medication reminders, offer food plans and fitness routines based on user data, and get reports and insights based on user data.

Who are we targeting?

Individuals with Diabetes: Our application is a comprehensive tool designed to assist individuals of all ages diagnosed with Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes, offering features for tracking blood glucose levels and medications, providing diet and nutrition advice, monitoring physical activity, and fostering a supportive community.

Caregivers and Family Members: Family members or caregivers of individuals with diabetes could also be potential users of the app. They may use it to help monitor their loved one's health, track medication schedules, and provide support and encouragement.

Healthcare Professionals: Our application is also an important resource for healthcare professionals such as doctors and nurses, enabling remote patient progress tracking, personalized recommendations based on collected data, and improved patient communication.

Health and Wellness Enthusiasts: People who are proactive about their health and wellness, even if they don't have diabetes themselves, may also be interested in using the app. They could use it to track their diet, monitor their physical activity levels, and access educational resources on nutrition and fitness.

MyGlooco offers:

Easy blood sugar tracking

Enter readings and choose your preferred unit (mmol/L or mg/dL).

Medication Scheduling

Receive alerts for your next medication cycle (insulin shots or oral medication).

Target setting and monitoring

Visualize your progress towards personalized blood sugar goals.

Health Assessments

Get insights based on your recorded data.

Diet and exercise

Suggest food plans and fitness routines based on user data.

Healthcare provider integration

Allow users to share their health data with the healthcare team monitoring them.
